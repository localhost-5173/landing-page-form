About the incubation centres

Where training turns into real work

Bano Qabil Incubation Centres are run by Alkhidmat Foundation Pakistan as part of the Bano Qabil program, Pakistan's largest free IT training initiative. Our centres in Bahadurabad and Clifton (Punjab Chowrangi) give young people a place to work on real projects, build a strong portfolio and take their first steps into jobs, freelancing and startups.

Bano Qabil has already trained more than 75,000 students. The incubation centres are the next step: you don't just learn, you do. You work in teams, get guidance from mentors, and leave with work you can show to any client or employer.

New · Accelerated Learning Program

Fast-track from course to career

The Accelerated Learning Program (ALP) is an intensive, mentor-led track at the Bano Qabil Incubation Centres for students who are ready to move faster. You learn, build and ship in one focused cycle, and finish with portfolio work and a clear next step.

Who it's for

  • Bano Qabil students and graduates who want to go deeper in their field
  • Learners who want real project experience before applying for jobs
  • Aspiring freelancers and founders who want mentor feedback on their work
  1. 01

    Skill sprint

    Focused, mentor-led sessions that close the gap between your course and what the industry expects.

  2. 02

    Build

    Join a team and work on a real project brief, with regular reviews from your mentor.

  3. 03

    Showcase

    Polish your work into portfolio pieces and present it to mentors and peers.

  4. 04

    Launch

    Step into a job, freelance work or your own startup, with support from Qabil.jobs and career coaching.

Requirements

Who can apply?

We welcome ambitious Bano Qabil learners who are ready to grow.

Bano Qabil students

Students and graduates of any Bano Qabil course, from any Karachi campus. Matric, Intermediate and university students are all welcome.

Required skills

The basics of your field from your Bano Qabil course, eagerness to learn, and a problem-solving mindset.

Willingness to Learn

Motivated individuals who are eager to learn, develop practical skills, and gain hands-on experience are encouraged to apply.

Other requirements

Able to attend on-site in Bahadurabad or Clifton for the full duration, with good communication skills and team spirit.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is an incubation centre?

A place where you use your skills on real projects with mentors, so you finish with real experience and a portfolio.

What is the Accelerated Learning Program?

An intensive, mentor-led track at the incubation centres that takes you from your course to real projects, a portfolio and your next career step: a job, freelance work or a startup.

Who can join?

Students and graduates of Bano Qabil courses from any Karachi campus. The application form asks for your course, teacher, campus and marks.

Where are the centres?

Bahadurabad (near Char Minar Chowrangi) and Clifton (Punjab Chowrangi), Karachi. See Our centres for directions.

How do I apply?

Click "Register Now" on this page and fill in the form. For questions, WhatsApp 0317-8226244 or visit banoqabil.pk.
